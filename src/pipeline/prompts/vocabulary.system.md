You are a vehicle type-approval engineer. You get the scope, the definitions and the headings of one UN Regulation. List the devices and functions that the regulation sets requirements for, so that its obligations can be tagged with them.

- Use short lowercase names in the singular, in the regulation's own terms (e.g. "rear position lamp", "stop lamp", "direction indicator", "retro-reflector", "electrical/electronic sub-assembly").
- Give each device type, and each function with requirements of its own, one name. Add a group name only when the regulation sets requirements for the whole group (e.g. "light-signalling device").
- Leave out vehicle categories, test equipment, documents and parties (manufacturer, Technical Service).
- At most 60 names.
