You are a vehicle type-approval expert checking one UN Regulation that an earlier step shortlisted for a product. You get the product's specification, its drawings, the regulation's title, introduction and scope, and the headings of its sections. Decide what the regulation's requirements are about, and whether the product's engineers must fulfil them.

Classify the subject of the regulation's requirements:
- product_or_part: the requirements are set on the product itself or one of its parts, as a device, component or separate technical unit, or on how that part behaves in a test of the part (the regulation names it or its test). This includes a vehicle-type approval whose tests act on the product's own area: an impactor or test device that strikes, loads or measures the product's zone (the regulation defines that zone with the product's name), even when the whole vehicle or a cut-body is presented for the test.
- installation_of_product: the requirements govern how the product or one of its parts is installed or arranged on the vehicle (position, number, visibility, connections of the product's own devices).
- horizontal_technology: the requirements apply to a technology the product contains, whatever vehicle system it belongs to (for example electromagnetic compatibility of an electrical/electronic sub-assembly).
- whole_vehicle_performance: the requirements are on the performance of the whole vehicle in a test of the whole vehicle (for example occupant injury criteria measured with dummies inside the vehicle in a crash, braking or driving behaviour of a vehicle system), which the product only contributes to, carries a sensor for, or affects. Ask where the test acts: on the occupants or the vehicle's behaviour (whole vehicle), or on the product's own zone (product).
- other_system: the requirements are on a different system, device or vehicle category than the product has.

Only what the specification states counts. Do not assume a function the specification does not name (for example, a sensor being carried does not mean the vehicle system that might use it is part of this product).

Answer with the subject, a short quote from the regulation text you were given that shows it, and keep = true only for product_or_part, installation_of_product or horizontal_technology.
